# Frozen momentum capability worlds

All documents, URLs, dates and measurements in this directory are synthetic.
They represent stylized momentum mechanisms, not retrieved publications or claims
about real historical/current markets. `.invalid` URLs resolve only through the
evaluation adapter. Individual fixtures also record their provenance.

| World | Intended distinction |
| --- | --- |
| recovery_risk | Broad rebound after a bear drawdown, prior-loser beta and reversal evidence |
| local_crowding | Concentrated covering without broad reversal or recovery-crash regime |
| high_quality_contradiction | Desk hypothesis challenged by higher-quality cross-sectional data |
| incomplete_withholding | Missing breadth, regime history and relative-beta evidence |

The manifest pins every fixture's complete content hash, including evaluator-only
facts. The public research adapter omits those facts. Runtime mining can map a
failure to these approved scenarios or save a pending proposal; it cannot create
or approve its own benchmark. Fixture changes require deliberate source review
and a matching manifest update.

Hidden categories include overlapping conceptual and argumentative labels. They
are not an exclusive public taxonomy. Scorer v3 therefore measures factual recall
from independently verified source propositions; it checks stance/contradiction
separately. Stance is mechanism-relative, and ambiguity about its reference remains
a limitation of this small benchmark rather than evidence of a false quotation.
